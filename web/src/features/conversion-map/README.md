# conversion-map

Signature visual: a ranked "ladder" of companies where each row draws one square per intern or working student seen (filled = went full-time), with the Count beside it.
Sort by chance (companies with under 10 people after the rest) or by volume, filter by country, and narrow to a college or field with a small GET form that re-runs the tool.
Supporting panels: conversion by country (20 squares per row, each 5%), the method text, and any other section rendered by the shared SectionView.
Thin or widened results show a warm explanation and a one-click "look again" widen button; rows under 5 people arrive pre-folded as "Other".
Ends with next-step buttons and the coverage line with the data date.
