# Internship chances

Shows how often internships and working-student roles turned into full-time jobs at the same company for people like the user.
Hero: the chance as a grid numeral (with its likely range) beside one square per intern, filled squares being the ones who converted, grouped by company with the target highlighted.
Below: the ranked table of companies (company, interns seen, went full-time, chance as a Count, target row inverted) and a plain "how we worked this out" panel.
Thin data (under 10 people) drops the percentage, explains why in plain words and offers to widen; any other section falls back to the shared SectionView.
Ends with the next-step button and the coverage and data-date line.
