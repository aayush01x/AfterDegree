# People like me (explorer)

Shows the cohort of people who share the user's undergrad school (or tier, or field) and experience band, and where they are now.
Top: a mentor-voice summary and the "who counts as like you" chips (match level and experience band; each re-runs the tool via its URL).
Middle: moved-abroad as a grid numeral beside the CohortWaffle by country, target selected (black), with a readout for any chosen country.
Below: tabs for roles, employers in the target country and further degrees (with where they studied); thin or widened cohorts get a plain explanation and a widen button.
Ends with the next-step button and the coverage and data-date line; any section the view does not specialise falls back to the shared SectionView.
